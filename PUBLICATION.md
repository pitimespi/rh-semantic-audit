## Status

The initial research prototype (v0.2) is published in the owner's standalone repository:
https://github.com/pitimespi/rh-semantic-audit

The initial publication contains 12 files: research reports, provenance, a review queue, source-level graph metadata, a Python static scanner, and four synthetic unit tests.
